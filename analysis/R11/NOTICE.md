# Provenance and scope

The new analysis scripts and manuscript are research work produced in this conversation. Human author attribution and final public-release licensing review remain pending.

`inputs/R10_handoff.zip` is an unchanged earlier conversation artifact; its own notices remain inside the archive. `inputs/R10_feedback_inner.zip` contains the user's previously submitted simulated-task records. Both are locked by SHA-256. Prior report copies are supporting context, not newly produced experiments.

`paper/spconf.sty` is the inherited transcription from an earlier manuscript. Its provenance comment is preserved; no claim of byte identity with a current official conference distribution is made. No font files are distributed.

References are citations to primary sources; the papers themselves are not reproduced. Input response metadata and provider labels do not authenticate model weights or invoices. The offline analysis opens no external account and contains no new paid command. It is not an operating-system security sandbox.
